# 05: Fulfill an order's Voucher lines after its Delivery succeeds

**What to build:** When an order's email Delivery becomes delivered, whatever started it, the plugin creates Medusa's fulfillment of the order's Voucher lines for the delivered quantities and marks it delivered, so the admin shows the order as delivered instead of waiting to be shipped. Lines that are not Voucher products are left to the store. The shipping option comes from `delivery.fulfillment.shippingOptionId` (read on each fulfillment), else the order's shipping method; its linked stock location is used. A fulfillment that fails is logged and leaves the Delivery delivered; the retry and recovery job of ticket 04 tries it again, for orders delivered in the last 7 days, without sending the email again, and skips canceled orders. Fulfillments carry `no_notification`. `delivery.fulfill` (default true) turns it off. The README recommends Voucher products without shipping and explains that an order whose Vouchers were delivered can only be refunded, not canceled. See "Fulfillment after a Delivery" and the following decisions in the delivery spec, and ADR-0005.
